Snap a Photo Instead of Writing Paragraphs

Broken chairs, faulty lights, and damaged floors often go unnoticed or get reported far too late. SnapFix lets students and staff upload a photo of the damage instead of writing a long, unclear description, so maintenance teams know exactly what they're dealing with.

📸

AWS Rekognition Does the Rest

Once a photo is uploaded, AWS Rekognition analyses the image and identifies the object involved - a chair, a light fixture, a sink, and so on - and suggests a damage category automatically. This cuts down on confusion between users and the maintenance team and speeds up repair time.
