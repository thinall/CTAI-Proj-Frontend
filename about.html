About SnapFix

Most people don't report a broken chair or a flickering light because it just feels like too much effort - finding the right form, figuring out who to email, writing a description that actually makes sense. SnapFix was built to remove that friction. See something broken? Snap a photo, and you're basically done - no long write-ups, no guessing which category it falls under.

AWS Rekognition looks at the photo, figures out what's in it, and suggests the damage category for you. That turns reporting a facility issue from a chore into something that takes less time than sending a text, while maintenance teams still get clear, consistent reports to work from instead of vague descriptions.
